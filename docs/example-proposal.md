# Modern Ecommerce Website for Acme Studio

Client: Acme Studio
Budget: $2,400.00 USD
Status: approved
Generated locally with Ollama (qwen2.5:0.5b); reviewed by the project owner.

Acme Studio needs a modern ecommerce website for their skincare brand. The site should include a product catalog, product detail pages, a shopping cart, and a secure checkout. The design should be clean and modern, with a focus on SEO and integration with their existing email marketing tool.

## Milestones

### 1. Requirements and Design — $480.00 (20%)
Define the sitemap and create responsive designs for the product catalog and detail pages.

Acceptance criteria:
- Client approves the sitemap and responsive product-page designs.

Not paid.

### 2. Implementation of Requested Features — $1,200.00 (50%)
Build the catalog, product detail pages, cart and checkout. Integrate the existing email marketing tool.

Acceptance criteria:
- The catalog, cart and sandbox checkout work on desktop and mobile. The email marketing integration passes a test signup.

Not paid.

### 3. Testing and Handoff — $720.00 (30%)
Test the website, configure basic SEO and give the client a content management walkthrough.

Acceptance criteria:
- SEO titles and descriptions are set. The client can update a product after the handoff walkthrough.

Not paid.

## Assumptions
- Client will supply product images, copy and branding, and provide access to hosting and email marketing accounts.

## Out of scope
- Post-launch maintenance, paid advertising and copywriting.
